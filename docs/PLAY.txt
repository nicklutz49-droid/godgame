GODGAME - how to play
=====================

A Black & White inspired god game. You are a hand in the sky; your village
worships you into power, and a rival god wants the island for itself.

STARTING
  Run godgame (godgame.exe on Windows). The title menu offers:
    CONTINUE            resume your newest save
    SKIRMISH            you vs the rival god (map: random island or an
                        authored map slot; difficulty: EASY / FAIR / CRUEL)
    SANDBOX             a peaceful island, no opponent
    EDITOR              sculpt your own island and save it as a map
    QUIT
  Arrows + Enter, or the mouse. Esc during play opens the pause menu
  (resume / save / load / volume / main menu / quit).

THE LOOP
  Villagers gather food and wood on their own. Drop a villager onto the
  totem to make a Worshipper - worship fills your MANA. Cast miracles
  (1-4 selects FOOD/RAIN/FOREST/FIREBALL, M casts; rain and forest need
  a Miracle Dispenser, fireball needs the Wonder) to feed, grow, and
  terrify. Belief grows when villagers WITNESS your acts, and belief is
  territory: your influence rings only reach where faith does.
  Win a neutral village by impressing it past 50% belief; win the war by
  taking every village the rival owns - its temple will fall.

THE HAND
  Left-drag ground        pan (grab the land)
  Left-drag a thing       pick up rock / tree / log / food / scaffold /
                          VILLAGER (they panic; that's belief too)
  release gently          set down. On trees/field/water/site = assign job;
                          on the totem = devote a Worshipper; scaffold on
                          scaffold = combine; scaffold on ground = build
  release while moving    throw (gifts hurled into a neutral village win
                          hearts when they land)
  Right/middle-drag       rotate & tilt camera
  Wheel                   zoom (holding a 3-stack: choose civic building)
  WASD / arrows, Q E      move / rotate camera (Shift = faster)
  1 2 3 4                 select miracle (food/rain/forest/fireball)
  M                       cast the selected miracle at the cursor

BUILDING
  Workshops turn wood into scaffolds. Combine stacks by gentle placement:
    1 Small Abode   2 Large Abode   3 civic (Store/Workshop/Creche/Graveyard)
    4 Field         5 Village Center upgrade   6 Miracle Dispenser   7 Wonder

KEYS
  F5 / F9 / F6    quick-save / quick-load / cycle save slot (in play)
  Tab             map editor (frozen time; 1-9 tools, [ ] brush size,
                  G owner, V village size, N blank island, L original
                  island as clay, F5/F9/F6 map slots)
  T               advance time of day        R   new random island
  F7              toggle sun shadows         F2  wireframe
  F8              original B&W meshes <-> placeholders (with --bw)
  F3 / F4         debug tint / sim speed     K / L  spawn villager / +10 res

COMMAND LINE
  godgame --seed 1234         a specific island
  godgame --map file.gmap     play an authored map
  godgame --load file.sav     resume a save
  godgame --no-rival          sandbox      --editor   boot into the editor
  godgame --match [days]      headless AI-vs-AI war report
  godgame --headless          run the self-test suite
  godgame --bw <dir>          wear your own Black & White install: original
                              islands (menu MAP: LAND 1-5 or --land N) and
                              original buildings/trees/rocks. Read at
                              runtime, nothing extracted or shipped.
  godgame --bw <dir> --bw-report   check the install, print an inventory

Personal project. Without --bw everything on screen is procedural
placeholder art; with it, the originals stay your own local overlay.
